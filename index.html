How it works

  1. Say or type a lineThe first line of any devaranama, in Kannada or English letters.
  2. Meet the singersSangati lists every singer who has posted that song on YouTube.
  3. Play right hereTap a singer to hear their version. Not the right one? Tap “Not this one”.

Why “Sangati”?

In Carnatic music, a sangati is one line of a song sung with its own variations. The same devaranama is sung differently by every singer and every tradition. Sangati puts those voices side by side, so you can hear a song the way your guru, your family or your favourite singer sings it.
